The brief

An electric delivery company, from the first decision to the bikes on the road.

QuickFleet runs same-day delivery on electric motorcycles, with battery-swap hubs across the city and a record for every parcel.

We worked on the business itself, then built everything a customer touches: the brand, the app and the website.

QuickFleet is a venture SOUL’s founders started together. The idea came from Sani and Emmanuel; we built it the way we build for every client.

02 — The idea

Three promises, on every parcel.

01

Fixed price

You see the price before you book, and it’s locked at pickup. Electric energy doesn’t move with the pump.

02

Known rider

Vetted, trained and named before they arrive. Our riders, on our machines.

03

Proof at the door

The receiver closes the delivery with a four-digit code. No app to download.

03 — The product

Follow one parcel.

Six moments in the customer app, from home to the door.

  1. 01 · Home

    Start from home.

    One button to send a parcel. Every rider is vetted, trained and tracked to the door.

  2. 02 · Where to

    Say where it’s going.

    Saved places and recent receivers come first, so a repeat delivery takes seconds.

  3. 03 · Price

    See the price. It’s locked.

    Route, distance and price before you book: ₦1,500, Garki to Maitama. It won’t move at pickup.

  4. 04 · Rider

    Know who’s coming.

    A named rider on a map, one minute away, with a number you can call.

  5. 05 · Proof

    The receiver closes it.

    They get a text link and confirm with a four-digit code. No app needed.

04 — What we built

A brand, a website and the network behind them.

Brand

A mark and palette made for trust: deep navy and purple, with teal and mint for everything electric. It runs from the bikes and boxes to the app and the site.

  • Deep#0C0730
  • Purple#22184A
  • Teal#0A997D
  • Mint#6FD9C1

Website

quickfleet.co explains the network the way a customer meets it: what it costs, who comes and how it’s proved. A waitlist sorts customers, businesses and riders.

quickfleet.co

Operations

Battery-swap hubs, three to five in every city, close to where deliveries start. A rider swaps a flat battery for a charged one in minutes, so a parcel never waits on a charger.
